Why It Matters

Your STR permit and business license may be separate requirements

Different jurisdictions can use different permit names, renewal cycles, application procedures, inspections, and business licensing requirements. Renewing one item does not necessarily renew everything connected with operating the property.

Northline tracks each requirement separately so clients can see what is current, what is due, and what needs action.

What to send us

  • Property address
  • City or county, if known
  • Whether you own the property or are considering purchase
  • Existing STR permit or registration information
  • Existing business or privilege license information
  • Any notices, application forms, or correspondence already received
  • Your goal and any deadline you are facing
